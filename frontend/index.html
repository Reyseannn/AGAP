FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an account to report?

Yes. A free account lets AGAP save your details, so each report only needs the problem and its location. Your account must be approved before you can file.

Who can see my report?

Only authorized barangay staff can see your report.

How long does a report take?

Processing time depends on the type and urgency of the concern. Barangay staff will review your report and update its status. You can check progress under My Complaints.

Is AGAP for emergencies?

No. For emergencies, call 911 or your barangay hotline.

Have a community concern?

Report it through AGAP and help your barangay respond faster.
