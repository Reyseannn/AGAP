HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from report to resolution

No forms to print and no lines to wait in.

1

Create your account

Register with your name, number, and address.

2

Report the problem

Pick a category and describe it. Your details fill in.

3

Barangay reviews

Authorized staff review the report and update its status.

4

Follow the progress

Check your report anytime until it is resolved.

WHAT YOU CAN REPORT

Choose the category that fits your concern

Not sure? Pick Other and describe what you see.

Road damage

Potholes, cracks, and damaged streets or sidewalks.

Flooding

Standing water or flooded areas in your neighborhood.

Drainage problems

Clogged, broken, or overflowing canals and drains.

Public facilities

Streetlights, barangay halls, courts, and parks.

Environmental concerns

Uncollected garbage, illegal dumping, and pollution.

Other community issues

Any other local concern that needs the barangay's attention.

REPORT STATUS

Pending

In Progress

Resolved

The concern has been addressed and the report is closed.

FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need an account to report?

Yes. A free account lets AGAP save your details, so each report only needs the problem and its location.

Who can see my report?

Only authorized barangay staff can see your report.

How long does a report take?

[To be set by the barangay.]

Is AGAP for emergencies?

No. For emergencies, call the hotline 0917 000 0000 or 911.

Have a community concern?

Report it through AGAP and help your barangay respond faster.
