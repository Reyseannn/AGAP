How AGAP handles your information

AGAP collects the details you provide to verify your account and manage your community complaints.

What we collect

Your name, contact details, address, verification ID photo, complaint description, location, and any photo you attach to a complaint.

How we use it

Barangay staff use these details to review your verification request, respond to reports, and update complaint status.

Who can see it

Your verification information and complaint details are available to authorized staff handling the system.

Keeping your account safe

Keep your password private and sign out when using a shared device. Contact the barangay if you believe your account has been accessed by someone else.

Contact

For questions about your information, contact your barangay office.
